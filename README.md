# Privacy Policy for BlogApp

Last Updated: May 2026

BlogApp values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Email address
- User-generated content such as blogs/posts
- Profile information
- Images uploaded by users (if applicable)

## How We Use Information

We use collected information for:

- Account authentication
- Account recovery
- Synchronizing user data
- Improving app functionality
- Displaying user-created content

BlogApp uses Supabase Authentication for secure account management.

## Local Storage

The app may store certain data locally on your device using Hive for offline access and improved performance.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure services to protect your information.

## Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion by contacting the developer or using in-app account deletion features if available.

## Third-Party Services

BlogApp uses:

These services may collect limited technical information necessary for app functionality.

## Children's Privacy
